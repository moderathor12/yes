Who We Are

A Generation That Refuses to Wait

Youth Environment Society (YES) is a youth-led organization dedicated to empowering the next generation of environmental advocates. We believe the time for action is now — not tomorrow, not after graduation.

Through education, community organizing, and direct environmental action, we create meaningful change in our communities while building the skills and networks that will shape a greener future.
